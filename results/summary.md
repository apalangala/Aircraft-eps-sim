## Battery sizing (dual generator failure, APU fails, 30 min to touchdown)

| Case | Minimum parallel strings | What limits it |
|---|---|---|
| BOL 25 degC | 15 | voltage |
| BOL -20 degC | 23 | voltage |
| EOL 25 degC | 19 | voltage |
| EOL -20 degC | 31 | voltage |

- Design: **74S31P**, 78 Ah, 20.9 kWh nominal, about **145 kg** including packaging.
- The 30 min profile only needs **8.1 kWh** at the terminals. The pack is 2.6 times bigger because a cold, aged pack cannot hold its voltage during the 25 kW landing peak.
- Keeping the pack at 25 degC (heater) would cut it to 19P, saving about 56 kg.

## Pulsed radar (50 kW bursts, 50 % duty, 10 Hz)

- Without a buffer the bus ripple is **29.4 V** (limit 6 V).
- A buffer with time constant **5.1 ms** meets the limit. It swings **255 J**, about **7.3 mF** of capacitance used between 300 and 400 V.

## Constant-power-load stability (radar PSU input filter)

- LC filter only: unstable above **20.3 kW** (ideal-source estimate 15.4 kW). The radar runs at 25 kW, so it would oscillate.
- With an R-C damping branch: stable across the 0-80 kW sweep. Minor-loop phase margin at 25 kW: **36 deg**, vector margin 0.54.
- The impedances still overlap near the 356 Hz generator/bus resonance, so Middlebrook's conservative condition is not met.
